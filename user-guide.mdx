# Withdrawing Money from an ATM

1. Take your bank card to an ATM and insert it, or tap it if the ATM supports contactless transactions.
2. Choose your preferred language, if prompted.
3. Enter your PIN, taking care to shield the keypad.
4. Select **Withdrawal** or **Cash Withdrawal**.
5. Choose the account to withdraw from, such as **Checking** or **Savings**, if asked.
6. Select a listed amount or enter the amount you want. Make sure it is within your available balance and any ATM limits.
7. Confirm the transaction. If the ATM displays a fee, review it before continuing.
8. Take your cash, card, and receipt. Some ATMs return the card before dispensing cash, so check the card slot before leaving.

Keep your PIN private. If the ATM does not return your card or dispense the cash, contact your bank using the number on its official website or your card.
